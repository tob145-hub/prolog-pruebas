**Sentencia (actualizada)**

**1. Decisión**: Sí, modifica. Dejo sin efecto la condena; absuelvo al conductor del delito de homicidio culposo (art. 84 CP) y de toda otra imputación. No se impone pena. Se dejan sin efecto inhabilitación y costas.

**2. Fundamentos**:
a) Materialidad: death, speed, red. But now prove cause: unforeseeable factory defect locked accelerator; caused speed; brakes insufficient.
b) In culpable homicide, not enough causal result. Must prove breach of duty of care, foreseeability/avoidability. Art. 18 CN and guilt principle. No strict liability.
c) The defect is a fortuitous event/force majeure that interrupts normative imputation. The fact that vehicle ran red loses reproach because he had no capacity to stop. No subjective reproach. It is not a case of "inobservancia de reglamentos" because the conduct was not within control. There is no "conducta alternativa adecuada".
d) No dolo or culpa. Even if residual questions over whether he could have used engine braking, the prosecution has not proven that and doubt favors acquittal.
e) Contrast: If defect had been known/foreseeable or he omitted possible reaction, could be negligencia. Not here.
f) Civil: acquittal doesn't preclude civil claims.

**3. Pena**: none. If detained, release.
